Need help with FaceAuth?

We're here to help.

FaceAuth isn't recognizing me

Make sure:

  • Your camera is connected and available.
  • FaceAuth has permission to access your camera.
  • Your face is clearly visible to the camera.
  • You have enough lighting for the camera to see you.
  • Your enrolled identity is still enabled.

You can always use your Mac password if FaceAuth doesn't recognize you.

FaceAuth isn't opening

Try:

  1. Quit FaceAuth.
  2. Open it again.
  3. Check that you're running a supported version of macOS.
  4. Make sure the application is installed correctly.

Camera access isn't working

Open:

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera

and make sure FaceAuth has camera access enabled.
