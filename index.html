Security starts with you

FaceAuth processes your face securely on your Mac, keeping recognition close to your device.

Private by design

Your face doesn't need to leave your Mac. FaceAuth is designed to keep your personal information where it belongs — with you.

Liveness Detection

FaceAuth checks that you're actually there — helping distinguish a real person from a photo or video before unlocking your Mac.

Local Processing

FaceAuth is designed to process face recognition locally, keeping your biometric information close to you instead of sending it to the cloud.

Software Requirements

System requirements

macOS Security — Gatekeeper May Block FaceAuth

  1. Try opening FaceAuth (glance) once.
    macOS may block FaceAuth (glance) the first time.
  2. Open System Settings on your Mac.
  3. Select Privacy & Security from the sidebar.
  4. Scroll down to the Security section.
  5. Look for a message saying “FaceAuth (glance) was blocked from use.”
  6. Click Open Anyway next to that message.
  7. If macOS asks for confirmation, confirm that you want to open FaceAuth (glance).
  8. Try opening FaceAuth (glance) again.
