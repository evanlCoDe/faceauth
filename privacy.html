Privacy Policy

Face ID for Mac.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how FaceAuth handles information when you use the Software.

1. Information FaceAuth Uses

FaceAuth may use information necessary to provide its authentication features, including:

  • Camera input
  • Face-recognition information
  • Enrolled identities
  • Application settings and preferences

The exact information processed depends on the features you use.

2. Face Data

FaceAuth uses face recognition to determine whether an enrolled identity matches the person in front of the camera.

FaceAuth is designed to process face-recognition information locally on your Mac.

We do not intentionally upload your face data to a remote server or cloud service.

Important: Only keep this statement if your actual implementation truly does not send face data or biometric information to your servers or third parties.

3. Camera Access

FaceAuth requires camera access to provide face-recognition features.

You can manage FaceAuth's camera permissions through macOS System Settings.

4. Data Collection

FaceAuth does not need to collect personal information from you to provide its core authentication features.

If your application collects analytics, crash reports, email addresses, IP addresses, or other information, those should be listed here specifically.

5. Third-Party Services

If FaceAuth uses third-party services, their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

If FaceAuth does not use third-party services, you can instead state:

FaceAuth does not use third-party cloud services to process your face-recognition data.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by FaceAuth. However, no software or computer system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

FaceAuth is not intended to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as FaceAuth develops. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
